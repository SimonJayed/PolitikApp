# Cabinet profile proposals

A read-only Supabase snapshot on November 27, 2026 contained 952 profiles. I matched the 44 entries in the existing executive roster by stored UUID, named office and identity, checking aliases against the full profile list. The 23 executive departments in the roster have **five matching sitting secretaries and 18 missing profiles**. The wider roster also has seven other matching officeholders and 12 missing candidates, excluding the President and Vice President. The existing 952 profiles and the presidential and vice-presidential exceptions are the baseline. Each proposed addition needs separate approval to expand that baseline.
